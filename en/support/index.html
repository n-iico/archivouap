Support the archive

This archive is free, carries no advertising and does not track its readers. Keeping it running costs little, but it does cost: domain, hosting and, above all, the time it takes to verify every source.

What it funds

Covering domain and hosting costs and the time spent maintaining the archive. No funding conditioned on coverage is accepted.

Independence

A donation grants no influence whatsoever over what is archived or how it is presented. The criteria are published in Methodology and are not negotiable: any contribution conditioned on the inclusion, exclusion or wording of content is declined.

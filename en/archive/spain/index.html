Context

The Spanish Air Force began declassifying its sighting files in 1991: 80 files, more than 1,900 pages, covering 1962 to 1995, which can now be consulted in digitised form at the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa. Anything after 1995 remains restricted. Since that collection does not publish updates in an indexable form, its microsite is monitored here in case new declassifications are added.

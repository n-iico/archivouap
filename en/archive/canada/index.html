Context

Through the Sky Canada Project, Canada's Office of the Chief Science Advisor examined how public reports of unidentified aerial phenomena are received and handled in the country; it released a preliminary report in January 2025 and the final report in July 2025. The project does not collect first-hand data — neither photographs nor testimonies — so it produces no case files: its official page is monitored here.

Official pages the system checks periodically for new publications. They do not offer their documents in a form that can be indexed one by one, so they are linked directly.

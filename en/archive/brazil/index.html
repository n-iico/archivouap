Context

The Arquivo Nacional's documentary collection on unidentified flying objects (BR DFANBSB ARX) holds hundreds of files dating from 1952, transferred by the Brazilian Air Force under directive 551/GC3. Its online catalogue requires human verification to consult, so it cannot be indexed automatically: what is monitored here is the Arquivo Nacional's press room, which announces each release.

Context

The Section for the Study of Anomalous Aerial Phenomena (SEFAA), within the Directorate General of Civil Aviation, has published periodic reports of analysed cases since 2018, succeeding in 2021 the CEFAA created in 1997. It is one of the few bodies in the world that releases its casework systematically and openly.
